Delete your Call Your Shot account

In the app (fastest)

Open the menu and tap Delete my account. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Fill in this form with the details on your account. We’ll match them to your account and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account details (display name, email, phone number, location and date of birth), your sign-in link, push token, picks, points, contest entries, messages and any unpaid prize claim. If you were paid a prize, we keep that prize record and the claim details we are required to keep for tax purposes, with the rest of your account anonymized. See the Privacy Policy.
