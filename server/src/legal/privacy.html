Call Your Shot Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Call Your Shot is a free sports pick’em game. You join free contests, make picks on real games with points that have no cash value, and the top player in a contest wins that contest’s prize. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Call Your Shot app and this website.

Information you give us

  • Account details: the display name you choose, your email address, your phone number, your country and state or province, and your date of birth. We use these to create your account, to keep each person to one account, and to confirm you are old enough and live where the contests are offered.
  • Sign-in: when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, your email address from that provider. We never receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Prize claims (winners only): to award a prize, we ask for your legal name, mailing address and date of birth. Canadian winners answer a skill-testing question. US winners whose prizes total $2,000 or more in value in a calendar year are asked for a Social Security number or taxpayer ID so we can issue the tax forms the law requires. Claim details are stored encrypted.
  • Messages: anything you send us through Contact us in the app.

Information created when you play

  • Game activity: the contests you join, your picks, your points, refills and forfeits, and your results and stats.
  • Notifications: if you allow push notifications, a push token for your device and the kinds of notifications you chose.
  • Technical information: our servers briefly see your IP address when your device connects. We use it only to keep the service working and to slow down repeated failed sign-in attempts. We don’t use it to find your location.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. There is nothing to buy in Call Your Shot.

Ads

Call Your Shot is free because players watch short video ads, for example to collect points or confirm a pick. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. On iPhone, we ask for permission before ads can use tracking; if you say no, you still get ads, but they aren’t personalized. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How we use information

  • To run the game: your account, contests, picks, points, leaderboards and stats.
  • To keep contests fair: one account per person, eligibility and age checks, and detecting fraud or abuse.
  • To award prizes and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To send the notifications you turned on, and to answer your messages.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

What other players see

Your display name and your game stats (such as your record, win percentage, best win streak, reloads and contest results) are public to other players. Your email address, phone number, date of birth, location and prize claim details are never shown to other players.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for anyone else’s marketing. We share it only:

  • with service providers that run the service for us: Render (hosting), Neon (database storage), Apple and Google (sign-in), Expo (push notifications) and Google AdMob (ads). They may use it only to provide their service to us;
  • to deliver a prize you won, for example with a shipping company;
  • when the law requires it, such as tax reporting for prizes, or to respond to valid legal requests;
  • to protect the safety and integrity of the game and its players, including investigating fraud.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. If you delete your account, we delete your account details, sign-in link, push token, picks, points, contest entries, messages and any unpaid prize claim. If you were paid a prize, we keep the record of that prize and the claim details we are required to keep for tax purposes, with your account otherwise anonymized.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app, from the menu: Delete my account.
  • Notifications: turn each kind on or off in the app, or turn them off in your device settings.
  • Ads: use your device’s ad settings as described above.
  • Access or correction: to ask for a copy of your information or to correct it, use Contact us in the app. Depending on where you live (for example California or Canada), you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, prize claim details are encrypted when stored, and access is limited to what is needed to run the game. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Who can play

Call Your Shot is for adults who live in the United States or Canada (except Quebec) and are at least the age of majority where they live. It is not meant for children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the changes are significant.
